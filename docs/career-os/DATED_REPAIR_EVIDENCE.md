## What it is

Application packages can link a completed, dated industrial repair role to an explicit numeric requirement for years repairing industrial processing/production equipment in an industrial environment (including the alternative military wording). The evidence retains the source dates and full duty summary.

## Why it exists

The previous mapper collected experience facts but used only exact skill-name matches. A supported repair-tenure requirement therefore remained a gap and its facts were omitted from the cover letter.

## How to use it

Prepare a new package version from the already confirmed master profile. Existing packages are preserved; generation policy v2 marks prior-policy packages stale through the existing version gate. Review the linked dates and source text. QA, candidate approval and export requirements still apply.

## Common problems

- Only one completed role with explicit month/year dates and affirmative manufacturing, equipment maintenance and repair duties qualifies. Unknown days are rounded down; ongoing/year-only dates and separate roles are not added together.
- Additional or different equipment/qualification conditions remain unsupported. This is not a general semantic equivalence engine.
- Skill keywords alone do not prove years of repair work. Negated, observation-only or training-only descriptions require review.
- Blueprint reading, technical English, Office, physical capacity, education, licences and employer-specific work authorization are not inferred by this rule. Any remaining mandatory gap still blocks approval/export.
- A generated draft is not a submitted application or an accepted release.
